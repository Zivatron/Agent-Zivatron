# Curated Research Report Format for Policy & Industry Analysis

## When to Use This Format
Use this report structure when the user requests:
- A **curated dossier** of articles, papers, or regulatory documents on a specific vertical[cite: 2].
- Research focused on **industry movements, legislative shifts, or technical breakthroughs**[cite: 2].
- Intelligence customized for a **specific organization or role** (e.g., executive briefing, advisory council, compliance team)[cite: 2].

---

## Report Template

```text
# [Topic] — Curated Research Report
**Topics:** [Key themes separated by commas]
**Target Audience:** [Organization/Role]
**Date:** [Current Date]

---

## Executive Summary
- Key finding 1 with source link
- Key finding 2 with source link
- Key finding 3 with source link

---

## 1. [Document / Source Title]
**Source:** [Publisher/Organization]
**Date:** [Publication Date]
**URL:** [Verified URL]

**Key Findings:**
- Specific data point, statutory requirement, or metric
- Core analytical insight
- Relevant limitation or requirement

**Strategic Relevance:** [1–2 sentences explaining direct impact on organizational strategy or compliance]

---

## 2. [Document / Source Title]
[Repeat structured layout above]

---

## Gaps & Verification Limits
- Unconfirmed parameters or pending policy announcements
- Jurisdictional or market-specific data omissions
- Conflicting timeline projections

## Recommended Actions
1. [Actionable next step]
2. [Actionable next step]

---
*Research conducted via multi-source verification. All URLs verified.*
```

---

## Curation Standards

- **Item Limit:** Deliver exactly 5 curated, high-signal items unless explicitly instructed otherwise[cite: 2].
- **Source Hierarchy:** Prioritize statutory bodies, peer-reviewed publications, and industry standards over general news aggregators[cite: 2].
- **Explicit Relevance:** Every entry must include a concrete section connecting the finding to the user's operational context[cite: 2].
- **Honest Gaps:** Explicitly document what could not be confirmed in the **Gaps & Verification Limits** section[cite: 2].
