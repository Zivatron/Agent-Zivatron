# Seasonal Species Profiles

## Spring / Summer
* **Primary Target:** Brown Trout (*Salmo trutta*)
  * **Thermal Zone:** 12°C – 18°C (54°F – 64°F)
  * **Feeding Window:** Late afternoon insect hatches (Mayfly / Caddis) and dawn streamer runs.
  * **Tactics & Gear:** 5wt fly rod, 4X–5X fluorocarbon tippet. Size 14–16 Parachute Adams, Elk Hair Caddis, or bead-head nymphs. Focus on bubble lines, riffle tails, and undercut banks.
* **Secondary Target:** Largemouth Bass (*Micropterus salmoides*)
  * **Thermal Zone:** 20°C – 28°C (68°F – 82°F)
  * **Feeding Window:** Early morning topwater bite; evening shallow weedline patrol.
  * **Tactics & Gear:** Medium-heavy spinning/baitcasting gear. Hollow-body frogs, Texas-rigged plastic worms, or spinnerbaits along lily pads and docks.

## Autumn / Winter
* **Primary Target:** Rainbow Trout (*Oncorhynchus mykiss*)
  * **Thermal Zone:** 8°C – 14°C (46°F – 57°F)
  * **Feeding Window:** Mid-day thermal peaks when water warms slightly under sunlight.
  * **Tactics & Gear:** Indicator nymphing rigs with midge patterns (Size 18–22) or slow-stripped Woolly Buggers through deep wintering pools.
* **Secondary Target:** Coastal Surf Fish / Mulloway (*Argyrosomus japonicus*)
  * **Thermal Zone:** 15°C – 20°C (59°F – 68°F)
  * **Feeding Window:** Night tides, specifically 2 hours before/after high tide during New or Full Moons.
  * **Tactics & Gear:** 12–14ft surf rod, 30lb braid, 60lb shock leader with 6/0–8/0 circle hooks on breakaway grapnel sinkers (4–6 oz). Fresh squid or fish fillets cast into deep gutters.
