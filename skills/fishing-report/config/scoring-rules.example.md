# Environmental Thresholds & Scoring Matrix

## 🚫 Hard Disqualifiers (RED LIGHT / Unfishable)
If ANY condition is met, mark day as Disqualified:
* **High Water Discharge / Flow Rate:** Exceeds 400 CFS (river too high / dangerous wading conditions)
* **Swell / Wave Height (Coastal):** Exceeds 2.2 m (7.2 ft)
* **Sustained Wind Speed:** Exceeds 25 knots (46 km/h) or gusts exceeding 35 knots
* **Severe Weather & Safety:** Lightning probability > 40%, flash flood warnings, or freezing air temperatures below -5°C

## 📊 Opportunity Matrix (10 Points Maximum)

### 1. Atmospheric & Wind Conditions (Max 3 Points)
* **3 pts:** Barometric pressure falling smoothly (1008–1014 hPa) AND wind speed < 10 knots from favorable direction (offshore/downstream)
* **2 pts:** Stable pressure trend AND light/variable wind < 15 knots
* **0 pts:** Rapidly rising pressure following cold front OR sustained onshore/unfavorable wind > 18 knots

### 2. Hydrological & Aquatic Metrics (Max 3 Points)
* **3 pts:** Flow rate in ideal band (150–250 CFS) / Swell height 1.0–1.6 m @ 8–11s AND water temp in target species optimal zone
* **2 pts:** Flow slightly elevated/low (100–149 CFS or 251–350 CFS) OR marginal water temperature
* **0 pts:** Blown-out river flow (> 350 CFS) OR flat/blown-out ocean surf OR extreme water clarity/muddy torrent

### 3. Solunar, Lunar & Tidal Exchange (Max 2 Points)
* **2 pts:** New Moon (~0.0) or Full Moon (~1.0) ± 0.08 synodic phase OR high tide/flow peak aligning with Major Solunar Feeding Window
* **1 pt:** Waxing/Waning Crescent or Gibbous phase OR Minor Solunar Feeding Window
* **0 pts:** Quarter Moons (Neap tides / low water displacement) with no active solunar window

### 4. Diurnal & Peak Window Timing (Max 2 Points)
* **2 pts:** Primary activity window (high tide, hatch peak, or feeding run) aligns within 2 hours of Dawn, Dusk, or Overcast Low-Light
* **1 pt:** Activity window occurs mid-morning or late afternoon
* **0 pts:** Peak activity window falls at mid-day under bright, direct sunlight
