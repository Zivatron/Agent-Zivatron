# Master Recipe Database Template (Menu.example.md)

> **Note for Users Installing the Food Skill:**  
> Save a copy of this file as `Menu.md` inside your designated food data folder (e.g., `/path/to/food_data/Menu.md`).  
> Populate this file with your household's recipes. The `food` agent skill parses this structure to select meals, scale ingredients (4–5 servings default), and generate consolidated shopping lists.

---

## 1. MASTER MEAL ROSTER

When generating a 7-day meal plan, prioritize **Tier 1 Favourites** for 30–40% of the week (1–2 meals), and select remaining meals from the general **Recipe Database**. Always cross-reference `State.md` to prioritize recipes that utilize expiring kitchen inventory.

## 2. RECIPE DATABASE

All recipes are scaled for 4–5 servings by default.

### Classic Spaghetti Bolognese
* **Source:** Family Favorite
* **Yield:** 4–5 servings
* **Ingredients:**
  * 500g lean beef mince
  * 1 brown onion, finely diced
  * 2 cloves garlic, minced
  * 1 jar (700g) passata or diced tomatoes
  * 2 tbsp tomato paste
  * 1 pack (500g) spaghetti
  * 50g Parmesan cheese, grated
  * 1 tbsp olive oil

### Roasted Chicken & Vegetables
* **Source:** Easy Weeknight
* **Yield:** 4–5 servings
* **Ingredients:**
  * 1kg chicken thighs or bone-in pieces
  * 6 medium potatoes, quartered
  * 3 carrots, cut into thick batons
  * 1 large red onion, cut into wedges
  * 2 tbsp olive oil
  * 1 tbsp dried Italian herbs
  * Salt and pepper to taste

### Mediterranean Chickpea & Feta Salad
* **Source:** Quick & Healthy
* **Yield:** 4–5 servings
* **Ingredients:**
  * 2 x 400g cans chickpeas, drained & rinsed
  * 1 cucumber, diced
  * 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
  * 150g feta cheese, crumbled
  * 1/2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted
  * 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  * 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
